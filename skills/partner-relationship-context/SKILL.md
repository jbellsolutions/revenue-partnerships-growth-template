# Partner Relationship Context

Use rel_search and identity resolution before rel_entity_upsert. Organization, person and typed partnership are distinct. Assign one relationship owner; link evidence with source references. Call rel_context before every draft and retain its digest. Record exact owner-supplied replies; record a send only after owner confirmation.
