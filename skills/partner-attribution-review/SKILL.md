# Partner Attribution Review

Read authorized source events and approved attribution rules. Match canonical partnership and transaction identities; dedupe and separate pipeline, booked calls, paid sales and reversals. Distinguish reported, provider-verified and estimated numbers. Hold conflicting credit for the owner. Return a reconciliation and exceptions packet. Never adjust disputed attribution or release payments.

Read AGENTS.md, the private program card and OPERATIONS.md for authority and receipt requirements. Secrets stay in the client secret store. Stop affected work when context, authority or connection evidence is missing. Report prepared versus completed work accurately.
