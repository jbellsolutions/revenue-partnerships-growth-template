# Affiliate Activation Support

Verify owner-confirmed acceptance and terms before onboarding claims. Prepare assets/disclosures, tracking requirements, owner assignments, launch milestone and support plan. Tracking provision is a separate approved action. Work from due open loops; help remove specific blockers. Measure first actual referral or campaign with source receipts. Complete with next owner, due date, outcome evidence or an explicit hold.

Read AGENTS.md, the private program card and OPERATIONS.md for authority and receipt requirements. Secrets stay in the client secret store. Stop affected work when context, authority or connection evidence is missing. Report prepared versus completed work accurately.
