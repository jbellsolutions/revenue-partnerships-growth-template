# Offering the system as a service

## What we deliver

A client gets a reviewed program strategy, a map of its addressable partner relationships, a functioning private context graph, a selected partner portfolio, activation playbooks and accountable operating work. The service does not promise hundreds of affiliates, a specific income or autonomous commercial decisions. A list export or a copied agent prompt is not the service.

Use one shared methodology and a separately governed application per client. Industry expertise, offers, restrictions and approval needs differ; the template is not a substitute for the intake interview.

## Three packages in the repository model

| Layer | Contains | Can be reused? |
|---|---|---|
| Core system | Public taxonomy, generic worker contracts, context schema, decision contracts, reviewed procedures, tests and recovery model | Yes, versioned and licensed |
| Client application template | Intake questionnaire, blank program card, family/type selection worksheet, dependency checklist, measurement and authority forms | Yes, with empty client facts |
| Private client instance | Actual offer/economics, audience and ICP, partner graph, contacts, transcripts, agreements, approval and provider receipts, credentials | No cross-client copying |

A learned improvement may return to the core only after it is generalized, stripped of client data, independently tested and approved within unchanged authority. An example from one client's audience is not proof that another client should target it.

## Client onboarding

1. Identify the business, its buyer journey and actual offer. Distinguish the client's customer ICP from its ideal partner profile and the individual partner decision-maker avatar.
2. Establish approved claims, commercial models, available fulfillment, human relationship owner and required approvals. Leave absent values unresolved.
3. Inventory current partners and channels through authorized read access; reconcile suppressions, existing producers and commitments.
4. Evaluate the complete taxonomy. Keep the reason for pursuing, researching, deferring or excluding each type. Define the first small opportunity cohort.
5. Create the private program card and graph. Import only approved records with source provenance; do not seed the graph with generated fictional “leads.”
6. Clone reviewed workforce templates into new identities with fresh account profiles. Bind that client's private connections and authority.
7. Prove research/context/draft behavior with synthetic data, then run an authorized real readback and bounded first activation experiment.
8. Operate from actual open loops and events; measure activation and productive partnerships, not background agent activity.

## Delivery milestones and artifacts

**Map:** program brief, all-family fit map, chosen partner types and opportunity hypotheses. **Research:** verified initial candidates, cards, overlap checks and partner-specific collaboration ideas. **Activation:** reviewed conversations, accepted partner plans and first promotion/referral support. **Operation:** source-event/CRM reconciliation, partner support, dormant relationships and performance review. **Improvement:** experiment versions and evidence, generalized template candidates and rollback record.

ClickUp tracks these service milestones and blockers. Attio holds the partner pipeline. The private vault explains why a relationship is in that pipeline and what happened. Every system must show the same event status without pretending a prepared artifact is a completed provider action.

## Replication gate

Test client A and synthetic client B with conflicting offers, commission assumptions, disqualifiers and identical partner names. B must have no A contacts, cookies, scope grants, memory, approval IDs or provider account bindings. It should share only public taxonomy and generic reviewed methods. Verify all external writes use B's explicitly chosen identity. Replaying an uncertain A action after transfer must require reconciliation.

The trial's current role exporter redacts obvious secret-shaped values, but free-text jobs and training may still contain client facts. Build clean templates from the generic package rather than assuming arbitrary employee export is safe to sell.

## Operational and commercial boundaries

Keep standard service terms, client-specific partner commissions and vendor/model spend as separate records. Commission examples from the taxonomy are research context only. Payouts, attribution adjustments, refunds and new spend are owner decisions. No new pricing, revenue guarantee or software/cloud provisioning is set by this design.

For migration from the earlier revenue agent, prepare an explicit retirement inventory: old instructions, schedules, outstanding tasks, relationships and uncertain effects. Preserve relevant Dream/affiliate/CRM context via reviewed import. Do not carry over generic LinkedIn content, Lead Radar jobs or OutFlo campaign permissions merely because a connector exists.
