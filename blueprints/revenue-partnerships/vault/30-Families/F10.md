# F10 Cause & Nonprofit Partners

Cause & Nonprofit Partners are charities, schools, teams, faith and civic groups where the business's contribution (a percentage of sales, a donation per unit, a fundraiser share or in-kind support) produces revenue, traffic or community access in return.
