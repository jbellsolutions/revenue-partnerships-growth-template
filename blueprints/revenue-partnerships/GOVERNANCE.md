# Charters, ownership and governance

## Director charter

**Mission:** grow the client's business through productive affiliates and partner relationships. **Accountable for:** program strategy, type coverage, partner qualification, research quality, relationship ownership, activation, support, next actions and verified reporting. **Inputs:** reviewed program card, taxonomy/type dossiers, sourced relationship context, authorized tool scopes and outcomes. **Outputs:** partner map, qualified opportunity portfolio, context-based drafts, activation plans, support actions, attribution exceptions and portfolio report. **Excluded:** general social publishing, Lead Radar operation, OutFlo campaign administration, unrelated prospecting and the other demand-generation flagships.

**Done means:** every selected relationship has supported fit, a clear owner and next action; every claimed external outcome has a receipt; every client-facing status distinguishes proposed, prepared, approved and completed work. A growing list of names is not program growth.

## Shared capability charters

| Role | Owns | Returns | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Family steward | Taxonomy definitions, model distinctions, dossier quality and family coverage | Type reviews and missing coverage | No independent partner contact or private portfolio fork |
| Partner-type specialist | Type-specific discovery, avatar/ICP application and qualification | Sourced candidates, opportunity briefs, exclusions | No invented reach, terms or actual leads; only chosen client types |
| Affiliate Manager | Recruitment, onboarding, activation, support and reactivation | Partner plans, approved asset requirements, stalled-partner recovery | Owner chooses acceptance and terms |
| Relationship Context Steward | Canonical identity, source facts, graph, restrictions and freshness | Bounded context brief, digest and committed state | Only relationship service writes protected/generated cards |
| Dream Portfolio Steward | High-leverage selection, research briefs and relationship accountability | Dream 100/500 dossiers and due actions | The client owner's own messages; no bulk campaign enrollment |
| Editorial / Evidence Reviewer | Claims, source support, tone, stale context and prohibited assertions | Accept/revise/hold decision with reasons | Independent of the originating draft; cannot authorize commercial effects |
| Attribution / Performance Reviewer | Source-event matching, conversions, pipeline and program reporting | Reconciled totals and held exceptions | Never decide disputed credit or release payouts |
| Attio CRM Operator | CRM identities, agreed pipeline, next actions, partner relationships | Provider IDs and verified synchronization receipts | Sole Attio writer; no invented touch, stage regression or unknown write retry |
| ClickUp Operator | Service delivery, project milestones, dependencies and blockers | Batched operating updates and real task links | Sole ClickUp writer; no prospect-per-task sprawl |
| Client owner / account lead | Brand, economics, partner acceptance, approvals and exceptions | Approved program, scopes, terms and decisions | Authority cannot be supplied by a model confidence score |

These are responsibility contracts, not a requirement to launch a separate permanent employee for every role. One director can prepare several artifacts; independent review must remain a separate review step with its own evidence, and a human resolves reserved decisions.

## Governance invariants

- Bind every job to `client_id`, `program_version`, `type_id` and relationship identity. Keep a canonical relationship owner even when several type profiles contribute research.
- Public program-page language is context, not approved commission terms. Illustrative economics and third-party benchmarks never become an agreement or guaranteed result.
- Separate research fact, inference, proposal, owner confirmation and provider confirmation. A prepared message is not a send; a booked-call suggestion is not a booking; a CRM packet is not a synchronized record.
- Research and private preparation can run within existing authority. Source Affiliate Manager uses draft-only Orgo execution. Preserve that boundary until the workforce has an independently enforced dispatch design and authorized scopes.
- Approvals bind the exact action/payload, approver, scope, expiry and version. Do not reuse approval after changing recipient, offer, channel or copy. Do not use a model's confidence as an approval.
- Store event intent before effect. If the effect is uncertain, stop that action, retain the reservation/receipt identity, reread the provider and reconcile. Never resend because a worker restarted.
- Dedupe by client + canonical person/org + channel and partnership motion. Affiliate subtype overlap must not become competing campaigns against the same person.
- Protect opted-out, restricted, recently engaged and already-producing partners; investigate prior commitments before preparing another ask.
- Client clones carry no contact graph, private commercial terms, permissions, browser sessions or trial budget resets. Credentials use the client's private connection process.
- Use current source schemas and domain-appropriate consent/data constraints. Do not collect special-category personal information merely to score partners.

## Review gates

**Research-ready:** program/buyer ICP reviewed; discovery sources authorized; private graph isolated. **Draft-ready:** real identity, sourced fit, fresh context and actual next ask. **Action-ready:** sender and tool access verified, restrictions clear, exact approved scope and independent receipt path. **Scale-ready:** first cohort produces supported outcomes, cost and error rates measured, unresolved duplicates/attribution held, adequate human review and support capacity. **Replication-ready:** template contains only generic/public context; a second synthetic client remains fully isolated.

Pause and escalation are scoped to affected work. A missing commission term blocks a proposed commitment; it need not stop public research. A failed CRM connection creates a pending sync packet, not a false-success dashboard. A verified opt-out blocks contact immediately. Emergency external-work stop remains effective without destroying the context graph.
